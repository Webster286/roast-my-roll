FAQ

It’s counting too few photos.
Check under Settings → Apps → roast my roll → Photos that “Full Access” is on. With “Limited Access”, the app only sees the pictures you’ve shared with it.

My album doesn’t show up.
The app shows albums with at least 10 photos. iOS doesn’t always hand albums inside folders or shared iCloud albums to apps – in that case, create the album once directly under “My Albums”.

How does duo mode work?
In the Photos app, open “People”, pick the person, select your photos together and add them to a new album. Then choose that album in roast my roll.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. The analysis runs entirely on your device. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I switch the language?
The app follows your iPhone’s language. To change it for this app only: Settings → Apps → roast my roll → Language.
